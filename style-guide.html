Body text runs in Inter at 16px/1.6, colored var(--text) (#525252) rather than pure black — softer against long paragraphs. A link uses the accent as its underline, which thickens on hover — the text itself stays ink, since accent-on-white text falls under the contrast minimum.

Alternate surface

A deliberate dark panel — opted into per-element with .surface-alt, never applied automatically by the browser or OS. #cfe5e6 on #12263f checks out at 11.6:1 contrast; the teal reads fine here (6.6:1) but stays off the light surface above, where it only clears 2.3:1.
