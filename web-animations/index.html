Web animations

Seven motion studies in one control shell: WebGL ring arrays, a frame-accurate video scrubber, cursor-reactive rings and a two-stage reveal. Every parameter is out on the table: open Parameters (P), pick a preset (1–4), or press H to hide the controls and just watch.
